Build a playful diary-style website for a funny UK book series called The Blended Diaries. The site should look like a diary, with lined-paper backgrounds, handwritten fonts for titles, and doodle accents. Tone: witty, warm, British, honest. Goal: showcase the series, capture emails, and link to Amazon KDP + Etsy shop.

Style & Branding

Background: soft cream notebook paper with faint horizontal lines.

Accents: doodles in margins (hearts, arrows, stars, paw prints, speech bubbles).

Headings font: handwritten style (Patrick Hand, Homemade Apple, or Shadows Into Light).

Body font: simple, clean (Roboto or Lato) for readability.

Buttons: look like sticky notes or washi tape strips.

Colours: cream paper, charcoal text, accents of coral, teal, mustard.


Pages

1. Home

Hero: tagline “Chaos. Love. Repeat.” with collage of book covers pinned like diary pages.

Hook: “Blended family life. Less Pinterest. More real.”

Series strip: Taylor, Caleb, Libby, Pumpkin — each card styled like a diary entry with cover + 2-sentence blurb.

Reviews: short quotes styled like scribbles in the margins.

Email signup: doodle arrow pointing to box “Get a free bonus chapter.”



2. Books

Grid of books on diary-style pages.

Each book subpage = open diary spread: cover on left, handwritten blurb + 3 funny quotes on right.

Buy buttons styled as doodle tape links.



3. About

Notebook page layout.

Short bio: “A rheumatology nurse turned chaos curator, writing honest, funny diaries about blended family life.”

Polaroid-style photo placeholder pinned with tape graphic.



4. Press & Reviews

Media kit download.

Short press blurb.

Contact form.



5. Freebies

Email signup for free sampler PDF.

Diary doodle design around signup box.



6. Contact

Sticky note style contact form with dropdown (Reader, Press, Collaboration, Schools).



7. Legal

Simple footer links: Privacy, Terms.
